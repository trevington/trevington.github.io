About Me

I am a sophmore mechanical engineering student at Iowa State University with a heavy focus on electric vehicle powertrains, electromagnetic simulation, and automated multidisciplinary design optimization.

Background & Interests

My work spans physical hardware fabrication and advanced computational modeling. I spend a lot of time bridging the gap between mechanical design and automated software routines—writing Python scripts to handle finite element analyses, configuring custom optimization workflows using OpenMDAO, and working on Formula SAE electric race car development.

Technical Focus Areas

  • EV Powertrains & Simulation: Battery thermal modeling, inverter integration, and motor drive topology design.
  • Automated Optimization: Gradient-based optimization algorithms (SLSQP) and parametric workflow scripting.
  • Electromagnetics & CAD: FEA modeling using Ansys Motor-CAD, PyMotorCAD, and FEMM, alongside mechanical modeling in Onshape.
  • Systems & Tooling: Custom keyboard layouts and typography workflows (including Shavian script integration via Keyman IME), structured note-taking in Obsidian, and advanced data pipelines using Python and VBA.
