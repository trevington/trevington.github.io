FRC Double Jointed Arm Concept

Published: September 14th, 2026

Overview

At the start of the 2025 FRC season I conceptualized a double-jointed arm design. While my team didn't pursue the design, in the offseason I explored the concept further. A 2 degree of freedom arm is cheaper and simpler than most elevator designs common in 2025.

Since the design couldn't reach the L4 pole (one of 4192's design criteria in 2025), we didn't pursue the double-jointed arm design.

Coaxialized arms are popular in robots due to the lessened mass and wiring on moving joints.

Design Criteria

The design should:

  • Minimize use of custom parts.
  • Be fabricated using common machinery (Mostly 3D Printed; CNC Router or Laser Cutter if absolutely neccessary).
  • Use a coaxialized wrist.
  • Adapt to play most pick and place games.

Using Onshape's derive tool I also made the design parametric. This allowed the design to be complete after a dozen or so hours of CADing.

Results

While I didn't complete the design before my freshman year of college began, the design concept did achieve the mechanical requirements.

Packaging the wrist and roller powertrains was more difficult than expected, and I ultimately was unable to keep the motors stationary (they still rotate with the first arm joint).

The design also used in-tube belt, the first time I've used it.

The rollers used geometry from Team 2910's 2025 robot. It was also the first time I used custom bevel gears on a design.
