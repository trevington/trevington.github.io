FRC 2025 Design

Published: September 16th, 2026

Overview

Reefscape was team 4192 Jaguar Robotics' 14th season. As team captain and design lead, I led our 35 person team to the world championship in Houston, ending a 4 year drought.

Our 2025 Robot Reveal.

Design Criteria

After designing several robot concepts, we landed on the final criteria for our robot.

Our Robot will:

  • Remove algae from the reef
  • Score Coral on three levels
  • Deep climb during endgame
  • Work every match

We also wanted to score algae in the processor to assist in reaching the Coral RP.
